Biomass Engineering English

Lesson 1 — Steam Explosion & Fermentation

Today's goal

Learn practical English for discussing biomass pretreatment, steam explosion and fermentation with engineers.

1. Key Vocabulary

WordEnglish中文
steam explosion
/stiːm ɪkˈspləʊʒən/
A biomass pretreatment process using high-pressure steam and rapid pressure release.蒸汽爆破：利用高压蒸汽并快速泄压，对生物质进行预处理。
pretreatment
/ˌpriːˈtriːtmənt/
A process used to prepare a material before the main process.预处理：在主要工艺之前对原料进行处理。
reactor
/riˈæktər/
A vessel where a chemical or physical process takes place.反应器；这里可理解为进行蒸汽爆破的压力容器。
high-pressure steam
/ˌhaɪ ˈpreʃər stiːm/
Steam maintained at a pressure higher than normal atmospheric pressure.高压蒸汽。
rapid pressure release
/ˈræpɪd ˈpreʃər rɪˈliːs/
A very fast reduction of pressure after steam treatment.快速泄压。
penetrate
/ˈpenətreɪt/
To enter something and move deeply into it.渗透；穿透；深入。
fiber structure
/ˈfaɪbər ˈstrʌktʃər/
The physical structure of the fibers in biomass.纤维结构。
cellulose
/ˈseljʊloʊs/
A major structural carbohydrate in plant cell walls.纤维素。
lignin
/ˈlɪɡnɪn/
A complex polymer that provides strength to plant cell walls.木质素。
heating value
/ˈhiːtɪŋ ˈvæljuː/
The amount of heat released when a fuel is burned.热值。
energy density
/ˈenərdʒi ˈdensəti/
The amount of energy stored per unit of mass or volume.能量密度。
carbon content
/ˈkɑːrbən ˈkɑːntent/
The amount or proportion of carbon in a material.碳含量。
black pellets
/blæk ˈpeləts/

white pellets
/waɪt ˈpeləts/
Conventional light-colored biomass pellets.白颗粒；传统浅色生物质颗粒。
fermentation
/ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən/
A biological process in which microorganisms convert substrates into useful products.发酵。
fermentation tank
/ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən tæŋk/
A vessel used to carry out fermentation under controlled conditions.发酵罐。
microorganism
/ˌmaɪkroʊˈɔːrɡənɪzəm/
A microscopic living organism, such as a bacterium or yeast.微生物。
culture medium
/ˈkʌltʃər ˈmiːdiəm/
A nutrient-rich material used to grow microorganisms.培养基；为微生物生长提供营养的介质。
sterilize
/ˈsterəlaɪz/
To kill or remove microorganisms so that unwanted microbes cannot grow.灭菌；杀死或去除微生物。
sterilization
/ˌsterələˈzeɪʃən/
The process of making equipment or material free of viable microorganisms.灭菌过程。
inoculation
/ɪˌnɑːkjəˈleɪʃən/
The process of adding the desired microorganisms to a culture medium.接种。
contamination
/kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən/
The presence of unwanted microorganisms or other impurities.污染；杂菌污染。

2. Important Sentences

“Steam explosion is a pretreatment process.”

蒸汽爆破是一种预处理工艺。

“High-pressure steam penetrates the biomass.”

高压蒸汽渗透进入生物质。

“Rapid pressure release breaks down the fiber structure.”

快速泄压会破坏/打开纤维结构。

“The tank is sterilized before inoculation.”

发酵罐在接种前进行灭菌。

“The culture medium provides nutrients for microorganisms.”

培养基为微生物提供营养。

“Sterilization helps prevent contamination.”

灭菌有助于防止污染。

3. Factory Visit Questions

“What is the steam pressure?”

蒸汽压力是多少？

“What is the residence time in the reactor?”

物料在反应器中的停留时间是多少？

“What is the moisture content before pelletizing?”

制粒前的水分含量是多少？

4. Important correction from today's discussion

不要把“黑色”直接等同于“更高热值”。
Black pellets can have different fuel properties depending on the raw material and process. A darker color can be associated with thermal treatment, but color itself does not create energy. For technical discussions, focus on moisture content, chemical composition, heating value and energy density.

5. One-minute speaking practice

“We use steam explosion as a pretreatment process. High-pressure steam penetrates the biomass. After a short residence time, the pressure is released rapidly. This changes the fiber structure and can improve the fuel properties of the biomass.”

我们使用蒸汽爆破作为预处理工艺。高压蒸汽渗透进入生物质。在短暂的停留时间后，压力快速释放。这会改变生物质的纤维结构，并可能改善其燃料性能。
