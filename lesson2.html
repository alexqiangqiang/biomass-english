Biomass Engineering English

Lesson 2 — Fermentation Basics & Factory Communication

1. Technical Vocabulary

fermentation /ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən/
发酵
A biological process in which microorganisms convert organic materials into useful products.
We use fermentation to produce liquid fertilizer and feed additives.
fermentation tank /ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən tæŋk/
发酵罐
A tank used to control and carry out the fermentation process.
The fermentation tank is equipped with temperature and pH sensors.
microorganism /ˌmaɪkroʊˈɔːrɡənɪzəm/
微生物
A very small living organism, such as bacteria or yeast.
We use microorganisms to start the fermentation process.
culture medium /ˈkʌltʃər ˈmiːdiəm/
培养基
A material or liquid that provides nutrients for microorganisms to grow.
The culture medium provides nutrients for microorganisms.
inoculation /ɪˌnɑːkjəˈleɪʃən/
接种
The process of adding a desired microorganism or culture to a medium.
The tank is sterilized before inoculation.
sterilization /ˌsterələˈzeɪʃən/
灭菌
The process of killing or removing unwanted microorganisms.
Sterilization helps prevent contamination.
sterilize /ˈsterəlaɪz/
灭菌（动词）
To kill or remove microorganisms, especially unwanted ones.
We sterilize the tank before inoculation.
contamination /kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən/
污染；杂菌污染
The presence of unwanted microorganisms or other unwanted materials.
We need to prevent contamination during fermentation.
aeration /eəˈreɪʃən/
曝气；通气
The process of supplying air or oxygen to a fermentation system.
Aeration is important for aerobic fermentation.
agitation /ˌædʒəˈteɪʃən/
搅拌
The process of mixing the material inside a tank.
Agitation helps keep the material evenly mixed.
nutrient /ˈnuːtriənt/
营养物质
A substance that microorganisms need for growth and activity.
The medium contains enough nutrients for the microorganisms.
pH /ˌpiː ˈeɪtʃ/
pH值；酸碱度
A measure of how acidic or alkaline a solution is.
How do you control the pH?
temperature /ˈtemprətʃər/
温度
A measure of how hot or cold something is.
The fermentation temperature is around 35 degrees Celsius.
residence time /ˈrezɪdəns taɪm/
停留时间
The amount of time a material remains inside a reactor or tank.
What is the residence time in the reactor?
batch fermentation /bætʃ ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən/
分批发酵
A fermentation process in which materials are charged into a tank and processed as one batch.
We use a batch fermentation process.

2. Factory Communication

Factory Visit
  • What type of fermentation process do you use?
  • Is it a batch process or a continuous process?
  • What is the fermentation temperature?
  • How long does the fermentation take?
  • How do you control the pH?
  • How do you prevent contamination?
  • How do you sterilize the fermentation tank?
  • What kind of microorganism do you use?

3. Useful Grammar — take + time

It takes + 时间 = “需要多长时间”。

It takes two hours.

It takes about 48 hours.

How long does the fermentation take?

4. Process Introduction

We use fermentation to produce liquid fertilizer and feed additives.

First, we prepare the raw material. Then we sterilize the tank and culture medium. After that, we inoculate the microorganisms and start the fermentation process.

5. Free Talk & Small Talk

No, I've been to Europe before, but this is my first time visiting your factory.

7. One-Minute Speaking Practice

Today I'd like to talk about fermentation.

We use fermentation to produce liquid fertilizer and feed additives.

We use a batch fermentation process.

The fermentation temperature is around 35 degrees Celsius.

The fermentation process takes about 48 hours.

We need to control the temperature and pH carefully.

We also need to prevent contamination.
