1. Technical Vocabulary

fermentation
/ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən/
发酵
the process of using microorganisms to convert materials into useful products
fermentation tank
/ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən tæŋk/
发酵罐
a tank used for fermentation
microorganism
/ˌmaɪkroʊˈɔːrɡənɪzəm/
微生物
a very small living organism, such as bacteria or yeast
culture medium
/ˈkʌltʃər ˈmiːdiəm/
培养基
a material or liquid that provides nutrients for microorganisms to grow
inoculation
/ɪˌnɑːkjəˈleɪʃən/
接种
the process of adding microorganisms to a culture medium
sterilization
/ˌsterələˈzeɪʃən/
灭菌
the process of killing or removing unwanted microorganisms
sterilize
/ˈsterəlaɪz/
灭菌
to kill or remove microorganisms
contamination
/kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən/
污染；杂菌污染
the presence of unwanted microorganisms or substances
aeration
/eəˈreɪʃən/
曝气；通气
the process of supplying air to a liquid or fermentation system
agitation
/ˌædʒəˈteɪʃən/
搅拌
the process of mixing a liquid or material
nutrient
/ˈnuːtriənt/
营养物质
a substance that provides nourishment for growth
pH
/ˌpiː ˈeɪtʃ/
酸碱度
a measure of how acidic or alkaline a solution is
temperature
/ˈtemprətʃər/
温度
the degree of heat in a substance or system
residence time
/ˈrezɪdəns taɪm/
停留时间
the amount of time a material remains inside a process or reactor
batch fermentation
/bætʃ ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən/
批式发酵
a fermentation process carried out in separate batches

2. Factory Questions & Useful Sentences

What type of fermentation process do you use?
/wʌt taɪp əv ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən ˈprɑːses duː juː juːz/
你们使用什么类型的发酵工艺？
A question about the type of fermentation process used.
Is it a batch process or a continuous process?
/ɪz ɪt ə bætʃ ˈprɑːses ɔːr ə kənˈtɪnjuəs ˈprɑːses/
这是批式工艺还是连续工艺？
A question asking whether the process is batch or continuous.
What is the fermentation temperature?
/wʌt ɪz ðə ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən ˈtemprətʃər/
发酵温度是多少？
A question about the operating temperature.
How long does the fermentation take?
/haʊ lɔːŋ dʌz ðə ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən teɪk/
发酵需要多长时间？
A question about fermentation duration.
How do you control the pH?
/haʊ duː juː kənˈtroʊl ðə piː eɪtʃ/
你们如何控制 pH？
A question about pH control.
How do you prevent contamination?
/haʊ duː juː prɪˈvent kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən/
你们如何防止污染？
A question about contamination prevention.
How do you sterilize the fermentation tank?
/haʊ duː juː ˈsterəlaɪz ðə ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən tæŋk/
你们如何对发酵罐灭菌？
A question about tank sterilization.
What kind of microorganism do you use?
/wʌt kaɪnd əv ˌmaɪkroʊˈɔːrɡənɪzəm duː juː juːz/
你们使用什么类型的微生物？
A question about the microorganisms used.
It takes two hours.
/ɪt teɪks tuː ˈaʊərz/
需要两个小时。
Used to state how long a process takes.
It takes about 48 hours.
/ɪt teɪks əˈbaʊt ˌfɔːrti eɪt ˈaʊərz/
大约需要48小时。
Used to state an approximate process duration.
We use fermentation to produce liquid fertilizer and feed additives.
/wiː juːz ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən tə prəˈduːs ˈlɪkwɪd ˈfaɪləzaɪər ænd fiːd ˈædətɪvz/
我们使用发酵来生产液体肥料和饲料添加剂。
A practical sentence for explaining production.
First, we prepare the raw material.
/fɜːrst wiː prɪˈper ðə rɔː məˈtɪriəl/
首先，我们准备原料。
The first step in describing a process.
Then we sterilize the tank and culture medium.
/ðen wiː ˈsterəlaɪz ðə tæŋk ænd ˈkʌltʃər ˈmiːdiəm/
然后我们对罐体和培养基进行灭菌。
A process-description sentence.
After that, we inoculate the microorganisms and start the fermentation process.
/ˈæftər ðæt wiː ɪˈnɑːkjəleɪt ðə ˌmaɪkroʊˈɔːrɡənɪzəmz ænd stɑːrt ðə ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən ˈprɑːses/
之后，我们接种微生物并开始发酵过程。
A process-description sentence.
The culture medium provides nutrients for microorganisms.
/ðə ˈkʌltʃər ˈmiːdiəm prəˈvaɪdz ˈnuːtriənts fər ˌmaɪkroʊˈɔːrɡənɪzəmz/
培养基为微生物提供营养物质。
Explains the function of the culture medium.
Sterilization helps prevent contamination.
/ˌsterələˈzeɪʃən helps prɪˈvent kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən/
灭菌有助于防止污染。
Explains why sterilization is important.
The tank is sterilized before inoculation.
/ðə tæŋk ɪz ˈsterəlaɪzd bɪˈfɔːr ɪˌnɑːkjəˈleɪʃən/
接种前对罐体进行灭菌。
Explains the sequence of sterilization and inoculation.
We use microorganisms to start the fermentation process.
/wiː juːz ˌmaɪkroʊˈɔːrɡənɪzəmz tə stɑːrt ðə ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən ˈprɑːses/
我们使用微生物启动发酵过程。
A simple explanation of the role of microorganisms.
How was your trip?
/haʊ wəz jʊr trɪp/
你的旅行怎么样？
A useful small-talk question.
It was great. This is my first time in Finland.
/ɪt wəz ɡreɪt ðɪs ɪz maɪ fɜːrst taɪm ɪn ˈfɪnlənd/
很好。这是我第一次来芬兰。
A natural travel and factory-visit response.
No, I've been to Europe before, but this is my first time visiting your factory.
/noʊ aɪv bɪn tə ˈjʊrəp bɪˈfɔːr bət ðɪs ɪz maɪ fɜːrst taɪm ˈvɪzɪtɪŋ jʊr ˈfæktəri/
没有，我以前来过欧洲，但这是我第一次参观你们的工厂。
Useful small talk when visiting a factory.
I'd like to check in.
/aɪd laɪk tə tʃek ɪn/
我想办理入住。
Useful at a hotel.
I have a reservation under the name Alex.
/aɪ hæv ə ˌrezərˈveɪʃən ˈʌndər ðə neɪm ˈæləks/
我用 Alex 的名字订了房间。
Useful at a hotel.
Could you tell me where the elevator is?
/kʊd juː tel miː wer ði ˈeləveɪtər ɪz/
请问电梯在哪里？
Useful in a hotel or building.
What time is breakfast?
/wʌt taɪm ɪz ˈbrekfəst/
早餐几点开始？
Useful at a hotel.
Which platform should I go to?
/wɪtʃ ˈplætfɔːrm ʃʊd aɪ ɡoʊ tuː/
我应该去哪个站台？
Useful when taking a train.
Does this train go to Helsinki?
/dʌz ðɪs treɪn ɡoʊ tə ˈhelsɪŋki/
这趟火车去赫尔辛基吗？
Useful at a railway station.
How long does the journey take?
/haʊ lɔːŋ dʌz ðə ˈdʒɜːrni teɪk/
旅程需要多长时间？
Useful when asking about travel time.
Today I'd like to talk about fermentation.
/təˈdeɪ aɪd laɪk tə tɔːk əˈbaʊt ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən/
今天我想谈谈发酵。
Opening sentence for a technical introduction.
We use a batch fermentation process.
/wiː juːz ə bætʃ ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən ˈprɑːses/
我们采用批式发酵工艺。
A concise factory-process sentence.
The fermentation temperature is around 35 degrees Celsius.
/ðə ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən ˈtemprətʃər ɪz əˈraʊnd ˌθɜːrti faɪv dɪˈɡriːz ˈselsiəs/
发酵温度大约是35摄氏度。
A useful process parameter sentence.
The fermentation process takes about 48 hours.
/ðə ˌfɜːrmənˈteɪʃən ˈprɑːses teɪks əˈbaʊt ˌfɔːrti eɪt ˈaʊərz/
发酵过程大约需要48小时。
A useful process-duration sentence.
We need to control the temperature and pH carefully.
/wiː niːd tə kənˈtroʊl ðə ˈtemprətʃər ænd piː eɪtʃ ˈkerfəli/
我们需要仔细控制温度和 pH。
A useful process-control sentence.
We also need to prevent contamination.
/wiː ˈɔːlsoʊ niːd tə prɪˈvent kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən/
我们还需要防止污染。
A useful contamination-control sentence.
