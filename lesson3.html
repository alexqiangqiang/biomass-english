Lesson 3 — Steam Explosion & Black Pellets

蒸汽爆破 · 黑色颗粒 · 工厂技术交流

本课根据你提供的 Valmet BioTrac / Power Wood 文章整理。重点不是逐句翻译，而是把文章内容变成你在欧洲工厂参观时可以直接使用的英文对话。🔊 点击播放按钮听机器朗读。
今天重点： steam explosion · continuous process · residence time · moisture content · energy density · mechanical strength · product consistency · grinding · combustion
#1
What kind of biomass do you use as raw material?
你们使用什么生物质作为原料？
#2
We mainly use fire-damaged wood and forestry residues.
我们主要使用火灾受损木材和林业剩余物。
#3
Why did you choose steam explosion technology?
你们为什么选择蒸汽爆破技术？
#4
We chose it because it can improve the properties of biomass pellets.
我们选择它，是因为它可以改善生物质颗粒的性能。
#5
What is the basic principle of the process?
这个工艺的基本原理是什么？
#6
We use high-pressure steam to heat the biomass and then release the pressure rapidly.
我们使用高压蒸汽加热生物质，然后快速释放压力。
#7
What happens to the biomass during rapid pressure release?
快速泄压时，生物质会发生什么？
#8
The water inside the biomass vaporizes rapidly, which opens up part of the fiber structure.
生物质内部的水分会快速汽化，从而打开部分纤维结构。
#9
Does steam explosion change the lignin structure?
蒸汽爆破会改变木质素结构吗？
#10
Yes. The heat softens the natural lignin, which can help bind the material during pelletizing.
会。热量会使天然木质素软化，有助于制粒时黏结物料。
#11
Do you need an external chemical binder?
你们需要额外的化学黏结剂吗？
#12
The softened natural lignin can provide part of the binding effect, so the need for external binders can be reduced.
软化后的天然木质素可以提供部分黏结作用，因此可以减少对外加黏结剂的需求。
#13
Is your process continuous or batch-based?
你们的工艺是连续的还是批式的？
#14
It is a continuous process with continuous feeding and discharge.
这是一个连续工艺，连续进料和出料。
#15
Why is continuous operation important for a large-scale plant?
为什么连续运行对大型工厂很重要？
#16
It helps keep the process conditions stable and reduces interruptions between batches.
它有助于保持工艺条件稳定，并减少批次之间的中断。
#17
What are the key process parameters?
主要工艺参数有哪些？
#18
The key parameters include temperature, pressure, residence time, moisture content, and feed rate.
主要参数包括温度、压力、停留时间、含水率和进料量。
#19
How do you control the moisture content of the raw material?
你们如何控制原料的含水率？
#20
We use drying and raw material blending to control the moisture content.
我们通过干燥和原料混配来控制含水率。
#21
What are the main advantages of the black pellets?
黑色颗粒的主要优势是什么？
#22
They have better water resistance, higher mechanical strength, and higher energy density.
它们具有更好的耐水性、更高的机械强度和更高的能量密度。
#23
Why is water resistance important?
为什么耐水性很重要？
#24
It reduces swelling, breakage, and material loss during storage and transportation.
它可以减少储存和运输过程中的膨胀、破碎和物料损失。
#25
How does higher energy density affect transportation?
更高的能量密度如何影响运输？
#26
It means we can transport more usable energy in the same volume.
这意味着在相同体积下，我们可以运输更多的有效能量。
#27
Can the black pellets be ground like coal?
黑色颗粒可以像煤一样研磨吗？
#28
They are designed to have grinding and combustion properties that are closer to coal.
它们的研磨和燃烧特性设计得更接近煤。
#29
Can existing power plants use these pellets?
现有电厂可以使用这种颗粒吗？
#30
It depends on the plant. We need to test the fuel in the actual mill and boiler system.
这取决于具体电厂。我们需要在实际的磨煤机和锅炉系统中进行燃料测试。
#31
What tests do you need before commercial operation?
商业运行前需要进行哪些测试？
#32
We need raw material tests, pilot tests, grinding tests, combustion tests, and emissions analysis.
我们需要进行原料测试、中试、研磨测试、燃烧测试和排放分析。
#33
How do you ensure product consistency?
你们如何保证产品的一致性？
#34
We continuously monitor moisture, temperature, pressure, particle size, bulk density, durability, ash, and heating value.
我们持续监测含水率、温度、压力、粒径、堆积密度、耐久性、灰分和热值。
#35
What is the main challenge with natural biomass?
天然生物质的主要挑战是什么？
#36
The raw material can vary in moisture content, ash content, and fiber composition.
原料的含水率、灰分和纤维组成可能会发生变化。
#37
How do you deal with this variation?
你们如何应对这种变化？
#38
We use raw material testing, blending, online control, and continuous quality monitoring.
我们通过原料测试、混配、在线控制和持续质量监测来应对。
#39
What is the goal of your project?
你们项目的目标是什么？
#40
Our goal is to produce a reliable low-carbon fuel that can replace part of the coal used by industrial customers.
我们的目标是生产可靠的低碳燃料，替代工业客户使用的一部分煤炭。
#41
What do you think is the most important advantage of this technology?
你认为这项技术最重要的优势是什么？
#42
For me, the key advantage is that it combines improved fuel properties with continuous industrial production.
对我来说，最大的优势是它把更好的燃料性能与连续工业化生产结合起来。

Speaking Practice

We are interested in steam explosion technology for biomass pretreatment.

We want to understand how the process changes the fiber structure.

We are also interested in continuous operation and product consistency.

Our main questions are about temperature, pressure, residence time, moisture content, and energy consumption.

We also want to know how the black pellets perform during storage, transportation, grinding, and combustion.
